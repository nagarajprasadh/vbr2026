Viksit Bharat Run 2026

Run to Serve the Nation

Suan Luang Rama IX, Bangkok

The Viksit Bharat Run is one of India's largest global outreach initiatives, uniting the Indian diaspora and local communities in a shared commitment to India's vision of becoming a fully developed nation by 2047. Organised as part of Seva Pakhwada — a fortnight dedicated to public service — the run embodies the spirit of Seva Bhav: service to the nation as a collective responsibility.

The Embassy of India, Bangkok brings the run to the heart of the city this year. Whether you are part of the Indian community in Thailand or a friend of India, join us on the morning of 30 September 2026 to run, pledge and celebrate together.

Seva Bhav
Service to the nation as a shared calling
Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam

Aatmanirbhar Bharat

Global Reach
Runs across 91 countries and 150 locations worldwide

Participants will take the Viksit Bharat pledge, connecting this morning's run to a movement of overseas Indians and their neighbours standing together for nation-building.
