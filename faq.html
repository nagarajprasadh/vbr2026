🏃 Viksit Bharat Run 2026

Event Guide · Wednesday, 30 September 2026 · Bangkok, Thailand

About the Run

Viksit Bharat Run 2026 is a nationwide initiative celebrating India's vision of becoming a developed nation by 2047. The Bangkok edition, organised by the Embassy of India, brings together the Indian community and friends of India for a 5 km run through the heart of the city.

🏅 Run Categories

▼
General (5 km)
Open to all participants aged 18–59. A timed 5 km run with prizes for the top 3 male and female finishers.
Senior (3 km)
For participants aged 60 and above. A non-timed, celebratory 3 km walk-run.
Junior (3 km)
For participants under 18. A non-timed 3 km run. Juniors must be accompanied by a registered adult guardian.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

▼
Is the event free?
Yes. Participation in the Viksit Bharat Run 2026 Bangkok edition is entirely free of charge.
Who can register?
The event is open to all — Indian nationals, Thai nationals, and international residents of Bangkok are welcome to participate.
Can I register a group?
Yes. Up to 5 participants can be registered per email address. For groups larger than 5, please use separate email registrations.
What do I bring on the day?
Your registration pass (printed or on your phone), a photo ID, and comfortable running shoes. Water will be available along the route.
Can I retrieve my pass after registering?
Yes. Use the "Retrieve your pass" link on the registration page. You will need the email address and year of birth used at registration.
What is the PDPA consent for?
Your personal data (name, email, T-shirt size) is collected solely for event coordination and communication, in compliance with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562. Data will not be shared with third parties or used after the event.
Who do I contact for queries?
Please write to the Embassy of India, Bangkok at the contact details provided on the Embassy website. Technical issues with registration can be flagged at the check-in counter on the day.
